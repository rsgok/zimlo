Signal, not activity.

One card. One decision.

Agent activity is infinite.
Your attention is not.

Zimlo is not another terminal, inbox, or dashboard. It is an editorial layer that keeps only what changes your judgment, your action, or your confidence.

01 / Editorial feed

See the signal.
Lose the transcript.

Zimlo edits agent activity into the few conclusions, decisions, and results that can change what you do next.

02 / Swipe to collaborate

Browse like TikTok.
Respond like X.

One focused card at a time. Swipe through important work, open the thread, approve, reply, or request a revision in seconds.

03 / Quiet by design

Your phone speaks only
when you are needed.

Approvals, failures, and results to review make the cut. Progress logs, heartbeats, and routine tool calls stay silent.

Local source of truth

The cloud connects.
Your Mac remembers.

Task text, code, and results never enter Zimlo Cloud. When your Mac is online, Cloudflare relays an already-encrypted connection. When it is offline, your phone shows its local cache and queues actions for reconnect.

Zero-command onboarding

Download. Open. Scan.
No bridge to understand.

You should never have to run zimlo start. Zimlo lives in the menu bar and explains each permission only when it matters.
